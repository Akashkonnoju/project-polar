ABOUT THE PROJECT

Why PolarConnect?

SIH RGT26063N — an integrated portal for polar science, knowledge, research, resources and discovery.
01

The Problem

Polar information is distributed across scientific repositories and can be difficult for students and general users to discover and understand.

02

The Solution

PolarConnect organizes knowledge, research, media, datasets, locations and trusted scientific sources in one searchable portal.

03

Objectives

Improve access to polar information, support education and research discovery, and make scientific content easier to explore.

PLATFORM

What users can do

Explore

Browse Climate, Ice & Glaciers, Polar Oceans and Biodiversity.

Search

Search across portal knowledge, research, media, locations, topics and datasets.

Discover

Open research resources and authoritative external scientific data sources.
